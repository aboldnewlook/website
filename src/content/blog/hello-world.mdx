This is the beginning of something new. I've wanted a place to write about the things I'm building, the problems I'm solving, and the ideas I'm chasing — and now I have one.

## What to expect

I'll be writing about:

- **Tools I'm building** — CLI apps, macOS utilities, and the design decisions behind them
- **Platform engineering** — patterns, opinions, and lessons from building developer platforms
- **Go** — the language I reach for most, and why
- **Open source** — contributing to and maintaining projects like OpenTDF

## Why now?

I've been building things for a long time, but I haven't always been great about sharing the process. This site is an attempt to change that — to think out loud, document what I learn, and maybe help someone else along the way.

If you're curious about any of the [apps I've built](/apps), or want to know more [about me](/about), those pages are live too.

More soon.
